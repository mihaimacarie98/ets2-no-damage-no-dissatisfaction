No Damage + No Passenger Dissatisfaction

--- Description ---
[h1]No Damage + No Passenger Dissatisfaction[/h1]
Keeps your truck and trailer damage at 0% and stops bus passenger satisfaction from ever dropping. Made for ETS2 1.62.

[b]Truck damage[/b]
[list]
[*] All collision damage zeroed (truck, trailer, dragged trailer, side and roof impacts)
[*] All mileage wear zeroed (engine, transmission, wheels, fixable and unfixable)
[*] Cargo damage zeroed
[*] Used trucks from the dealer spawn with no wear
[/list]

[b]Bus passenger satisfaction (Bus DLC)[/b]
[list]
[*] All satisfaction penalties zeroed: harsh acceleration, crashes, speeding, part wear, late arrival, traffic offences
[*] New passengers start at maximum satisfaction
[*] Acceleration job-failure disabled
[/list]

[b]Notes[/b]
[list]
[*] Damage or wear already in your save is not reset. Repair once at a service station.
[*] Do not enable other "No Damage" mods at the same time.
[*] Unofficial, not affiliated with SCS Software.
[/list]
